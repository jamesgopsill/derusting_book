# About the Author

Hi, I'm James Gopsill — an Associate Professor (Reader), based in the Design and Manufacturing Futures (DMF) lab at the University of Bristol. My research sits across engineering design, complex systems, and — increasingly — what happens when you let AI agents loose on supply chains and manufacturing systems.

`derusting` is where that research meets a fairly hands-on habit of actually building the things I study. A lot of my work over the years has circled the same question from different angles: what happens to a system's resilience, ownership, and trust when you stop routing everything through a single, central point of control? Additive Manufacturing turned out to be a great testbed for that — real machines, real jobs, real consequences if the "central server" goes down — which is exactly what `derusting` and the wider decentralised manufacturing research it comes from are built to explore.

Outside of this book, I maintain a handful of other open-source tools in the same space — [egcode](https://github.com/jamesgopsill/egcode) (end-to-end gcode encryption), [binarygcode](https://github.com/jamesgopsill/binarygcode) and [meatpack](https://github.com/jamesgopsill/meatpack) (Rust ports of Prusa's and OctoPrint's gcode formats), [embassy-buddy](https://github.com/jamesgopsill/embassy-buddy) (a board support crate for the same Buddy board this book is built on), and a set of TypeScript clients for talking to various 3D printer APIs. See [Other Projects](./projects.md) for the full list.
